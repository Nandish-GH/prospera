About Prospera.

Prospera helps middle school students explore academic subjects and career pathways early, through workshops that are clear, interactive, and built for younger learners.

Our mission

We introduce students to subjects, skills, and career pathways they are often not exposed to until high school.

Our vision

We want students to feel more prepared, informed, and excited about their future educational and career opportunities.

How we work

Each workshop is interactive, beginner-friendly, and activity-based, with presentations, discussions, simulations, and Q&A.

Why it matters

Early exposure builds confidence, makes advanced subjects feel approachable, and helps students discover what interests them sooner.

Core values

Accessibility, curiosity, leadership, innovation, and community guide every workshop and partnership.

Community partners

We work with libraries, schools, community centers, and youth organizations to bring learning into more spaces.
