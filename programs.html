Four core curricula

Programs and subjects.

Each track is designed as a beginner-friendly introduction to a field, pairing content knowledge with practical activities and discussion.

Business / Economics

How value, markets, and decisions work

Students explore basic business thinking, money concepts, pricing, tradeoffs, and entrepreneurship through simple case studies and classroom activities.

Computer Science

How technology solves problems

This track introduces computational thinking, logic, digital systems, and the mindset behind coding, building, and problem solving.

Law / Speech & Debate

How to speak clearly and argue fairly

Learners practice public speaking, persuasive structure, evidence use, and respectful disagreement while learning how legal thinking works.

Medical Sciences

How the body, care, and health systems connect

Students gain a foundational look at anatomy, medicine, health careers, and the curiosity needed to understand science in everyday life.

Workshop structure

1. IntroPreview key ideas and vocabulary.
2. ActivityUse a hands-on challenge or discussion.
3. ReflectionConnect the lesson to school and careers.
