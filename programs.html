Four core subjects

Programs and subjects.

Each track introduces middle school students to a field through approachable content, discussion, and hands-on learning.

Business / Economics

Foundational financial and economic awareness

Students learn absolute and comparative advantage, visual merchandising, supply and demand, entrepreneurship, and marginal analysis so they can build practical financial knowledge early on.

Computer Science

Computational thinking and innovation

This track introduces coding fundamentals, problem-solving, artificial intelligence concepts, technology applications, and the logic behind computational thinking.

Law / Speech & Debate

Communication, persuasion, and civic understanding

Learners develop public speaking, debate structure, critical thinking, persuasive communication, and legal and civic understanding to build confidence and leadership.

Medical Sciences

Health, anatomy, and scientific inquiry

Students explore human biology, basic anatomy, healthcare careers, medical innovations, and scientific inquiry through hands-on learning and guided exploration.
