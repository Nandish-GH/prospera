Mission and vision

We make advanced subjects feel approachable, practical, and exciting for young learners.

Mission

Expose students early.

Prospera is dedicated to exposing middle school students to subjects, skills, and career pathways they are not typically introduced to until high school.

Vision

Build a future-ready generation.

We want students to feel more prepared, informed, and excited about their future educational and career opportunities.

What makes the program work

Every workshop is interactive, beginner-friendly, engaging, activity-based, and focused on real-world learning.

Presentations and discussion

Students get a clear introduction to each subject before moving into guided questions and conversation.

Games and simulations

Workshops use challenges, role-play, and simulations to make complex ideas approachable and memorable.

Group activities and Q&A

Students collaborate, apply what they learn, and finish with time to ask questions and connect the lesson to future opportunities.

Upcoming announcements

This space will highlight workshop dates, tabling events, registration windows, and partner-hosted sessions.

Introductory Exploration Session

Free session for students and families to preview the workshop format and core subject areas.

Community workshops

Prospera partners with libraries, schools, and youth organizations to run educational workshops and outreach events.

Long-term growth

The organization is building toward expanded workshop offerings, student leadership opportunities, and accessible educational resources.

Long-term goals

Prospera aims to expand workshop offerings across multiple communities, build partnerships with schools and libraries, develop student leadership opportunities, and inspire future academic and career interests among younger students.
